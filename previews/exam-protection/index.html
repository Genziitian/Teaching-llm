How to claim and use your coupon

  1. Submit a protection claim, select the discount benefit and upload your official result proof.
  2. After approval, the manager manually creates or selects a coupon and assigns it to your account.
  3. Open My Coupons and scratch the issued coupon to reveal its code.
  4. Go to genziitian.in/courses and select the same course your coupon is for. For example, a Python coupon can only be used for Python.
  5. Sign in with the same email address you used here to claim the coupon. The discount is linked to that account.
  6. Copy your revealed coupon code and apply it at checkout for that course.
  7. Complete the purchase within 7 days of scratching, subject to the coupon’s eligibility and remaining uses.
Once issued by the manager, your coupon appears here.

08

The details that keep it fair.

Important conditions at the moments students and managers need them.

When do protection and claims disappear from dashboards?

Student dashboard: 60 days after the protection purchase or claim submission. Manager dashboard: 360 days after that record’s purchase or submission. Each record has its own timer; reviews, notes and fulfillment do not restart it. Claim records are hidden from dashboards without being deleted. Only uploaded proof media is deleted 60 days after claim submission, including replacement uploads; marks, reasons, messages, notes and refund/coupon records remain. Issued coupons follow their own expiry and usage rules.

What happens when the manager closes the service?

Students see Closed instead of Add Protection. View Details stays available so they can read all rules and benefits. Existing protection, eligible Outcome A claims and manager claim processing remain available. Reopening the service restores Add Protection for unprotected courses.

Does an approved claim mean the money has been refunded?

No. Approved confirms the benefit decision. Processing means the manager is arranging it. Refund completion is recorded only after the manager has actually issued the refund manually.

Can a screenshot be automatically rejected?

No OCR or automatic proof rejection is part of this workflow. A manager reviews the official results-dashboard screenshot and can ask for better proof or additional information.

What if a coupon is expired, used up or for a different course?

Checkout must validate the assigned student, eligibility, expiration date, usage limit and course or subject scope. A failed check prevents application and shows the reason. A Quiz 1 discount is 1.5× the protection fee paid, rounded to the nearest whole rupee, toward the next purchase of that same course.

Who can see internal notes and proof?

Internal notes are visible only to authorized managers. Students see their own claim, submitted proof and public manager updates. Real app and website permissions must enforce those boundaries on the server.

Which policy decisions are still needed before launch?

Publish the protection purchase deadline and claim-submission window. This walkthrough also prevents duplicate claims in the current demo session; production duplicate and appeal rules need an explicit policy.
